refactor: Centralize Realtime logic in ProjectKanbanView

To enable broadcasting from multiple child components (`KanbanBoard`
and `TaskDetailSheet`), the Realtime subscription and broadcast
logic has been lifted into the shared parent, `ProjectKanbanView`.

- `KanbanBoard` is refactored to receive `onUpdate` as a prop.
- `TaskDetailSheet` now accepts and calls `onUpdate` after a task
  is successfully modified.
- This change ensures a consistent Realtime update mechanism for
  both task movement and task detail edits.
